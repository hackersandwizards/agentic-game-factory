## Regenerating the example art

The game art rebuilds with `bun scripts/game-art.mjs` in `studio/` (see the studio skill), every
cleanup step included. The title picture was made once:

- `workshop.webp`: `studio/greeting/assets/workshop-hw-960.png`, the video's picture on its 960x540 art
  grid, as lossless WebP (`cwebp -lossless`), pixel-identical to it.
